## Description (4000 chars max)

Ayllu is a comprehensive, offline-first field research companion for iOS. Designed for archaeologists, anthropologists, conservation biologists, and CRM professionals, it replaces multiple separate tools with a single app that works without network connectivity.

Record GPS waypoints with precision coordinates in decimal, DMS, or UTM formats. Capture geotagged photos, take field notes with on-device voice-to-text transcription, and record GPS tracks with distance and elevation statistics. Download map regions for offline use with OpenTopoMap tiles.

Key Features:

- GPS Waypoints: Record locations with automatic coordinate capture, categories, custom tags, and metadata. Navigate to saved waypoints with distance and bearing.

- Custom Data Forms: Design custom data collection forms with 9 field types including text, numbers, dropdowns, dates, GPS coordinates, and more. Validate data with required fields, range checks, and pattern matching.

- Hierarchical Records: Define custom record types that mirror your research methodology (Site > Trench > Context > Artifact). Navigate data with breadcrumb paths and tree views.

- Area Recording: Draw polygons and polylines on the map by tapping vertices or GPS-tracing boundaries. Calculate area and perimeter automatically.

- Offline Maps: Download map regions for use in remote fieldwork areas. OpenTopoMap tiles show terrain, trails, and land features.

- Field Notes: Write text notes linked to projects and waypoints. Record voice notes with on-device speech-to-text transcription that works completely offline.

- GPS Tracks: Record breadcrumb trails with live statistics including distance, duration, elevation gain, and current speed.

- Data Import & Export: Import GPX, CSV, and GeoJSON files from other tools. Export waypoints to GPX, records to CSV, and geometries to GeoJSON.

- Data Quality Dashboard: Monitor data completeness with per-type progress indicators showing which records are missing required fields.

- Project Management: Organize all fieldwork by project with statistics, date tracking, and search.

All data is stored locally on your device. No accounts, no cloud services, no tracking. Your research data belongs to you.

## App Review Notes

Ayllu is an offline-first field research app. Key information for review:

1. No account or login is required. All features are immediately available after onboarding.

2. Location permission is requested for GPS waypoint recording and track recording. The app records precise coordinates for research data collection.

3. Camera permission is requested for capturing geotagged field photos linked to research waypoints.

4. Microphone and Speech Recognition permissions are requested for voice-to-text field note transcription. All speech processing happens on-device using Apple's SFSpeechRecognizer -- no data is sent to external servers.

5. The app is designed to work completely offline. Core features (waypoints, notes, photos, data forms) function without any network connection. Network is only needed for initial map tile downloads.

6. No analytics, tracking, or third-party services are included. No data leaves the device unless the user explicitly exports it.
